# Expense and Travel Policy

Northwind Robotics reimburses reasonable business expenses that are submitted correctly and on time.

## Submitting Expenses

All expenses are submitted through Ledgerly, the company expense tool. Receipts are required for any single expense over $25. Expenses must be submitted within 30 days of the purchase date; late submissions require written approval from a director.

Reimbursements are paid with the next payroll run after approval, which is normally within 10 business days.

## Approval Limits

Managers can approve individual expenses up to $2,500. Expenses between $2,500 and $10,000 require director approval. Anything above $10,000 requires approval from the VP of Finance before the purchase is made.

## Travel

Book all flights through the company travel portal, TripDesk. Economy class is standard for flights under six hours. For flights of six hours or longer, employees may book premium economy.

Hotel stays are reimbursed up to $250 per night in standard cities and up to $350 per night in high-cost cities. The list of high-cost cities is maintained in TripDesk and currently includes New York, San Francisco, London, Zurich and Tokyo.

Personal car use for business travel is reimbursed at the current IRS standard mileage rate.

## Meals

When travelling, meals are covered up to $75 per day, including tips. Alcohol is not reimbursable, except at client dinners approved in advance by a director.

## Home Office and Equipment

Each employee has a one-time home office stipend of $500, available during their first 90 days, for items such as a chair, desk or monitor. Laptops and peripherals are issued by IT and are not purchased through expenses.

## Learning and Development

Employees have an annual learning budget of $1,500 for courses, books, certifications and conference tickets. Conference travel is paid from the team travel budget, not the learning budget.
