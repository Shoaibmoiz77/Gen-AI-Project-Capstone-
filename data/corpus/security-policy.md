# Information Security Policy

Everyone at Northwind Robotics is responsible for protecting company and customer data.

## Accounts and Passwords

Passwords must be at least 14 characters long. Use the company password manager, Vaultline, to generate and store them. Never reuse a work password on another site.

Multi-factor authentication (MFA) is mandatory on every company account. Hardware security keys are required for engineers with production access and for anyone in Finance; everyone else may use an authenticator app. SMS codes are not an accepted second factor.

## Devices

Company laptops must have full-disk encryption enabled and must lock automatically after 5 minutes of inactivity. Operating system security updates must be installed within 7 days of release.

Report a lost or stolen device to the security team within 1 hour by posting in the #security-help channel or emailing security@northwind.example. The device will be remotely wiped.

## Data Classification

Company data falls into four classes: Public, Internal, Confidential and Restricted. Customer telemetry from robots is classified as Restricted. Restricted data may only be stored in approved systems and must never be pasted into external tools, including public AI chatbots.

## Phishing

If you receive a suspicious email, use the "Report Phish" button in the mail client rather than forwarding it. The security team runs simulated phishing tests every quarter.

## Training

All employees complete security awareness training within their first two weeks and then once a year.

## Access Reviews

Managers review their team's access to production systems every quarter. Access for departing employees is revoked on their last working day.
