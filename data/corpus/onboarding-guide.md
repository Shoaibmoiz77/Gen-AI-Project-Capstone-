# New Hire Onboarding Guide

Welcome to Northwind Robotics. This guide covers your first few weeks.

## Before Day One

Your laptop ships to your home address about one week before your start date. You will receive an email with instructions to set up your accounts and the password manager.

## Your First Week

Day one starts with a welcome session at 9:30 local time, followed by IT setup. Each new hire is paired with an onboarding buddy from a different team, who is your go-to person for questions during your first 90 days.

By the end of your first week you should have met your manager for a goal-setting conversation and completed the mandatory compliance modules in the learning platform.

## Engineering Onboarding

Engineers ship a small change to production in their first two weeks. Production access is granted only after completing security training and receiving a hardware security key.

## Working Hours and Location

Core collaboration hours are 10:00 to 15:00 in your team's primary time zone. Northwind Robotics is hybrid: employees within 50 miles of an office are expected on site on Tuesdays and Thursdays. Fully remote roles are marked as such in the offer letter.

## Benefits Enrollment

You must enroll in health benefits within 30 days of your start date. If you miss this window, you will have to wait for the next open enrollment period in November, unless you have a qualifying life event.

The company matches 401(k) contributions dollar for dollar up to 4% of salary, and matching contributions vest immediately.

## Probation and Reviews

There is no formal probation period. Your first performance check-in is at 90 days, and full performance reviews take place twice a year, in April and October.
