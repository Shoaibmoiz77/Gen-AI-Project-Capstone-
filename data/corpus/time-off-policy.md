# Time Off Policy

This policy applies to all full-time employees of Northwind Robotics. Part-time employees receive benefits pro-rated to their scheduled hours. Northwind Robotics is a fictional company used to demonstrate this project.

## Paid Time Off

Full-time employees accrue 20 days of paid time off (PTO) per calendar year, accrued evenly each pay period. After five years of continuous service, the annual accrual rises to 25 days.

Up to 5 unused PTO days may be carried over into the next calendar year. Carried-over days expire on March 31 if they have not been used. Unused PTO beyond the carry-over limit is forfeited on January 1.

PTO requests of three or more consecutive days must be submitted in the HR portal at least two weeks in advance. Requests of one or two days need only manager approval in the portal before the day is taken.

## Sick Leave

Sick leave is tracked separately from PTO. Every employee receives 10 paid sick days per year. Sick days do not carry over.

A doctor's note is required only for absences longer than three consecutive working days.

## Parental Leave

Birthing parents receive 16 weeks of fully paid parental leave. Non-birthing parents, including adoptive and foster parents, receive 12 weeks of fully paid leave. Parental leave can be taken in up to two blocks and must be used within 12 months of the birth or placement.

## Company Holidays

The company observes 11 paid holidays per year. The office is also closed for the winter shutdown between December 24 and January 1, which does not count against PTO.

## Volunteer Day

Each employee may take one paid volunteer day per year to work with a registered nonprofit. Log it in the HR portal under the "Volunteer" category.
