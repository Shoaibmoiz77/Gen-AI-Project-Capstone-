# Kestrel X2 Product FAQ

The Kestrel X2 is Northwind Robotics' autonomous warehouse inventory robot. It scans shelf barcodes and RFID tags and reports stock levels in real time.

## Specifications

The Kestrel X2 has a top speed of 2.0 metres per second and a maximum scanning height of 9 metres using its telescoping mast. It weighs 48 kg and can operate in temperatures from -10°C to 40°C, which makes it suitable for most cold-storage facilities.

## Battery and Charging

The battery provides up to 10 hours of continuous operation. A full charge on the X2 Dock takes 90 minutes. The robot returns to the dock on its own when its battery falls to 15%.

## Navigation and Safety

The X2 uses lidar and stereo cameras to build and update a map of the warehouse. It stops within 0.5 metres when a person steps into its path. The robot is certified to ISO 3691-4, the safety standard for driverless industrial trucks.

## Connectivity

The robot connects over Wi-Fi 6 and can fall back to a private 5G network. Inventory data syncs to the Northwind Cloud dashboard every 60 seconds.

## Warranty and Support

Each Kestrel X2 includes a 3-year limited warranty covering parts and labour. Batteries are covered for 2 years. Customers on the Premier support plan receive next-business-day on-site repair; standard support is handled remotely.

## Software Updates

Firmware updates are released monthly and installed automatically during the customer's configured maintenance window. Customers can defer an update by up to 14 days.

## Pricing

The Kestrel X2 is sold as a subscription at $1,200 per robot per month, which includes software, cloud access and standard support. The Premier support plan is an additional $300 per robot per month.
